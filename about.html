Who We Are

Badariya Flowers has been crafting genda & toran decorations, jhoomars, latkans and haar malas for weddings, festivals and everyday devotion. Every piece is handmade with care, blending traditional craftsmanship with designs that suit modern celebrations. From single pieces to bulk wholesale orders, we help our customers decorate their homes, venues and temples with artificial flowers that look and feel premium — without the maintenance of fresh flowers.
